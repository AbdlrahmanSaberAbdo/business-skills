# Grill the plan

Use when a decision is fuzzy or a plan has holes the user hasn't noticed: the platform, the returns policy, the name, the launch offer, the supplier. Works with or without a hub doc.

## Steps

1. If there's a hub doc, read it fully. Never ask what it already answers.
2. Name the decision in one line, and what "decided" looks like.
3. Ask **one question at a time**: 2–4 concrete options, a one-line trade-off each, and a recommendation when there's a sensible default. Wait for the answer.
4. Go breadth-first: surface every sub-decision before digging into one. Keep a running list of what's still open.
5. Name any conflict with what's already decided the moment it appears ("easy exchange" vs "no returns"), and let the user choose.
6. Stop when the decision fits in one or two sentences, or the rest needs facts from outside (hand those to **research-options**).
7. Record the result with **to-brief**: settled answers into the Brief, research questions into Open decisions.

## Rules

- Never ask two questions in one message.
- Look up anything that changes (prices, features, regulations) before offering options, and date it.
- Legal, tax or financial questions: give sourced facts, say you're not a lawyer or financial advisor, and name what to confirm with one.
